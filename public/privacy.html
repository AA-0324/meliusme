Privacy Policy for MeliusMe

Effective Date: December 31, 2026

Last Updated: December 31, 2026

Melius ("Melius," "we," "us," or "our") operates the MeliusMe mobile application and related services (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information you provide directly

We may collect information you choose to enter into the App, including:

  • Name or nickname
  • Age
  • Sex or gender preference
  • Height and weight
  • Body measurements
  • Nutrition goals
  • Meal logs
  • Food photos you choose to save
  • Settings and preferences
  • Notes, templates, and other content you create
  • Support messages you send to us

B. Information stored locally on your device

MeliusMe is designed as a local-first application. Your meal data, profile data, goals, settings, and related app content are stored on your device unless you choose to export or share them. Depending on your device and App settings, some data may be backed up through your device's native backup system.

C. Information collected automatically

We may automatically collect limited technical information about your device and App usage, such as:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • Crash or error information, if enabled
  • Usage events within the App
  • Purchase status and entitlement state, if applicable

We do not use the App for advertising tracking.

D. Purchase and entitlement information

If you purchase Pro features or other paid features, payment processing and entitlement verification may be handled by third-party services or app store billing systems. These providers may receive transaction details necessary to process your purchase.

If you use RevenueCat for purchase and entitlement management, RevenueCat may receive end-user technical information and end-user transaction information, including device details, Apple receipt files, or Google Play information as needed to manage your entitlements.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App
  • Store and display your nutrition logs, goals, streaks, and preferences
  • Generate summaries, charts, progress views, and other insights
  • Enable Pro features if you have purchased them
  • Process and verify purchases and restore entitlements
  • Respond to support requests
  • Improve the App's performance, reliability, and usability
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, or misuse

3. Sensitive Personal Information

Some information you may enter into MeliusMe may be considered sensitive personal information under applicable privacy laws, including health-related information, body measurements, and nutrition-related data.

We use sensitive personal information only for App functionality, security, purchase handling, and legal compliance. We do not use it for advertising, cross-context behavioral advertising, or unrelated commercial purposes.

4. California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you may have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the CPRA, including the right to know, delete, correct, opt out of sale or sharing, and limit use of sensitive personal information.

We do not currently sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. If that changes, we will update this Privacy Policy and provide the required opt-out disclosures.

You may submit California privacy requests by contacting us at the email listed below. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

5. Other U.S. State Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have privacy rights under other U.S. state privacy laws. If your state law gives you rights to access, delete, correct, or opt out of certain processing, you may contact us at the email below to exercise those rights.

6. GDPR / UK GDPR Information

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process personal data only where we have a valid legal basis, which may include performance of a contract, legitimate interests, compliance with law, or your consent.

We use your personal data to provide the App, manage your device-level settings, process purchases, restore entitlements, provide support, maintain security, and comply with law.

If you are in the EEA or UK, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing, and request portability of your personal data, subject to applicable law. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. International Data Transfers

If personal data is transferred from the EEA or UK to a processor or service provider in the United States, we rely on appropriate transfer safeguards, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Data Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this Policy or as required by law. Our general retention periods are:

  • Local app data on your device: Until you delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall the App
  • Support emails and support records: 24 months after your last contact
  • Purchase and entitlement records: As long as needed to provide access and resolve disputes, and for up to 7 years where needed for accounting or legal compliance
  • Diagnostic or error logs, if enabled: 90 days
  • Exported files you create: Until you delete them from your device or storage location

9. Local-First Data Storage

MeliusMe is designed so that your core nutrition and profile data remain on your device. We do not maintain a central cloud database for your personal meal logs or profile data unless a future feature explicitly requires it.

This means:

  • Your meal history stays on your device
  • Your profile and body metrics stay on your device
  • Your settings stay on your device
  • Your local data is controlled by you

If you delete the App or clear your device storage, local data may be permanently lost unless you exported a backup first.

10. Children's Privacy

MeliusMe is not intended for children under 13. For users in the EEA and the United Kingdom, MeliusMe is not intended for users under 16.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the applicable age threshold. If you believe a child has provided us information in violation of this section, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Security measures may include:

  • Local encryption for sensitive data, where implemented
  • Limiting third-party services
  • Minimizing external tracking
  • Reducing centralized storage of personal data

You are responsible for protecting access to your device. If someone has access to your unlocked device, they may be able to view data stored locally on that device.

12. Your Choices and Controls

You may:

  • Edit your profile and nutrition data in the App
  • Delete meal entries
  • Change settings and goals
  • Export your data, if the feature is available
  • Uninstall the App to remove local data from your device
  • Contact us to request help with your data

If you want to remove all locally stored data, you may need to clear the App's storage from your device settings in addition to uninstalling the App.
