Privacy Policy

MeliusMe

Effective date: November 10, 2026
Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MeliusMe handles information when you use the MeliusMe meal and nutrition-tracking application and its public web version (the “App”). “Melius,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to Melius, the trade name used by Akshar Arvind, the individual operator of the App.

This notice describes the current implementation of MeliusMe. The App is designed to keep meal and profile information on your device. There are limited exceptions for technical information handled by the services that host the App and for purchase information handled by RevenueCat and any checkout provider when purchase functionality is configured.

At a glance: MeliusMe does not require an account and does not sync your meal history, profile, or food photos to a Melius cloud database. Those records are processed and stored locally. The current code includes a RevenueCat purchase integration; when configured and used, it sends purchase-related and technical information to RevenueCat. Hosting providers may also process ordinary connection logs.

1. Information the App Handles

A. Information you enter or create

Depending on the features you use, information stored by the App on your device may include:

  • Profile information: a name or display name and an optional profile image.
  • Optional body-profile information: age, height, weight, a sex selection, unit preference, and a selected goal. These fields support optional in-app calculations and may be considered health-related or sensitive personal information.
  • Meal information: meal type, date and time, calorie and nutrient values you enter (including protein, fiber, and sugar), tags, and a food photo if you choose to attach and save one.
  • Other app information: water-intake entries, nutrition goals, meal templates, display and accessibility preferences, streaks, challenges, levels, progress information, and related app settings.
  • Consent record: onboarding choices and a timestamp recorded when you confirm the age, Terms of Service, and Privacy Policy prompts. This record is stored locally.

You decide what optional profile and meal information to enter. The App does not require a name, body-profile information, or a food photo to use its basic features.

B. Camera and photo-library access

If you choose to take a meal photo, the App asks the device or browser for camera access. The current camera implementation requests video only and does not request audio. You may instead choose an image using the device's file picker. The App processes the selected image in the browser; if you save it with a meal, the photo is stored as part of that meal's local record.

The current source code does not send meal photos to a Melius server or to a remote food-recognition or AI-analysis service. The App does not request access to your entire photo library; you select the image through the system file picker. Your device or operating system may separately process permissions and file access.

C. Information handled by hosting providers

When you load the hosted App or this public policy page, the relevant hosting, content-delivery, and security providers may process ordinary connection information needed to deliver and protect the pages. This can include your IP address, browser or device information, requested page or file, request time, and related server or security logs. The App's current source does not use this information to build an advertising profile. The App is currently served from meliusme.lovable.app, and the public policy pages are served from GitHub Pages.

D. Purchase and entitlement information

The source includes the RevenueCat Web SDK for optional Pro purchase and entitlement functionality. When RevenueCat is configured for a build and you use purchase-related features, RevenueCat may receive an anonymous App User ID generated for that installation, device or browser technical information, and purchase or entitlement information needed to present products, process a transaction, verify access, or restore the local purchase state. Purchase information may include transaction records and platform receipts or purchase tokens where relevant.

The App stores the generated RevenueCat identifier and its local entitlement cache on your device. There is no MeliusMe login or account-linking system. The current integration does not pass your meal logs, food photos, body-profile fields, or nutrition goals to RevenueCat.

If a checkout is enabled, the payment processor or app-store provider used for that checkout handles payment details under its own privacy policy. MeliusMe does not directly collect or store your complete payment-card number. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

E. Messages you send to us

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and the contents and attachments of the message you choose to send. We use that information to respond and handle the request. Your email provider also processes the message under its own terms and privacy policy.

2. How Information Is Used

Information stored in the App is used to provide the features you choose, including recording meals and water intake, displaying history and progress, saving preferences, calculating optional nutrition estimates, and maintaining local challenges and streaks.

Purchase-related information is used by RevenueCat and the applicable checkout provider to present and process purchases and check purchase entitlements. Contact information is used to respond to messages. Hosting providers use connection information to deliver, maintain, and protect the hosted pages.

The current version of the App does not include an advertising SDK, an in-app analytics SDK, or a crash-reporting service. We do not use meal, photo, or body-profile information for advertising, and we do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

3. Local Storage and Security

MeliusMe is local-first. Meal records, saved meal photos, profile details, goals, water entries, settings, and progress data are stored in browser or app storage on the device rather than in a Melius-operated cloud database. The core tracking features do not require an account or a network connection after the App has loaded.

New meal records and app values written through the current encrypted-storage code are encrypted using the browser's Web Crypto API with AES-GCM and a 256-bit key before being saved to IndexedDB. The encryption key is generated on the device as a non-extractable CryptoKey and stored in a separate IndexedDB database on the same device and app origin. Decryption takes place locally when the App needs to display or use the information.

Some older installations may contain records created before encryption was introduced. The App attempts to migrate those records to encrypted storage. If a migration cannot be completed and verified, the existing legacy value is retained rather than intentionally deleted. For that reason, we cannot promise that every legacy record on every older installation has already been encrypted.

Encryption helps protect stored records against casual inspection, but it is not a substitute for device security. The key is stored on the same device as the encrypted data, and the App does not require a separate encryption password. Someone who can use your unlocked device, access its browser profile, or execute malicious code in the App's origin may be able to access information while the App is in use. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

4. When Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share meal logs, saved food photos, or body-profile information with advertisers. Information may be processed by the following service providers in the limited circumstances described above:

  • Hosting and content-delivery providers: receive connection and request information when they serve the App or public policy pages.
  • RevenueCat: receives purchase-related and technical information when its integration is configured and purchase functionality is used. It does not receive meal or body-profile records through the current integration.
  • Payment processors or app-store providers: process payment and transaction information when you complete a purchase through a checkout or store they operate.
  • Email providers: process messages you choose to send to our support address.

If you export a CSV file or choose to share information using your device's sharing features, the file or information is provided to the destination you select. The current CSV meal export contains meal dates, times, types, nutrition values, and tags; it does not include saved food-photo files or your body profile. The receiving app or service then handles the information under its own policies.

We may also disclose information if required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the App.

5. Retention and Deletion

  • Local App information: remains in the device's app or browser storage until you delete it, clear that storage, or the operating system or browser removes it. Melius does not keep a separate server-side copy of local meal, photo, or profile records.
  • Exported files: remain wherever you save or share them until you delete them.
  • Support emails: are kept for as long as reasonably needed to respond, handle follow-up, and maintain appropriate business records, subject to applicable law.
  • Purchase and hosting records: are retained by the relevant service providers under their own retention policies. We do not control those providers' independent retention practices.

You can delete individual meal entries using the App's available controls. The current App does not provide user accounts, cloud meal-history storage, or a single in-app control that erases every category of local data at once. To remove all MeliusMe data stored for the App's origin, clear the App's storage or the browser's site data for MeliusMe in your device or browser settings. On devices using a web-app wrapper, clearing the wrapper's app data may also be necessary. Uninstalling a wrapper by itself may not clear browser-managed site data.

Because there is no MeliusMe account system, there is no account to deactivate or delete. Clearing local storage removes the local records and the locally stored encryption key; once the key is removed, any remaining copies of encrypted records may no longer be readable. You should export any information you want to keep before clearing storage. Delete exported copies separately from the locations where you saved or shared them.

6. Children's Privacy and Age Requirements

The App's onboarding screen states that users must be at least 13 years old, or at least 16 years old in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom. MeliusMe is not intended for children below those applicable age thresholds. We do not knowingly permit use by children below the stated thresholds. If you believe a child below the applicable age has provided information through the App or contacted us, please email us so we can review the situation.

For questions or requests involving information handled by Melius, RevenueCat, or another provider, contact us using the details below. However, hosting, purchase, payment, and email service providers may process technical or transaction information in countries other than the country where you live. Those providers handle such information under their own privacy notices and applicable data-transfer requirements.
