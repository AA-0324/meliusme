Privacy Policy for MeliusMe

Effective Date: June 18, 2026

Last Updated: June 18, 2026

Melius ("Melius," "we," "us," or "our") operates the MeliusMe mobile application and related services (the "App"). Melius is the trade name used by Akshar Arvind, who is the owner and operator of the App. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use the App.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information you provide directly

We may collect information you choose to enter into the App, including:

  • name or nickname
  • age
  • sex or gender preference
  • height and weight
  • body measurements
  • nutrition goals
  • meal logs
  • food photos you choose to save
  • settings and preferences
  • notes, templates, and other content you create
  • support messages you send to us

B. Information stored locally on your device

MeliusMe is designed as a local-first application. Your meal data, profile data, goals, settings, and related app content are stored on your device unless you choose to export or share them. Depending on the version of the App, this data may be stored using local storage technologies such as localStorage or IndexedDB, and may be encrypted locally before storage if enabled in the App.

C. Information collected automatically

We may automatically collect limited technical information about your device and App usage, such as:

  • device type and operating system
  • App version
  • purchase status and entitlement state, if applicable
  • crash or error information, if enabled
  • usage events within the App

We do not use the App for advertising tracking. We do not currently operate analytics or crash-reporting services in the App unless specifically disclosed in a future version of this Policy.

D. Purchase and entitlement information

If you purchase Pro features or other paid features, payment processing and entitlement verification may be handled by third-party services or app store billing systems. These providers may receive transaction-related information, purchase tokens, receipts, device information, and other technical data necessary to process purchases and restore access.

If you use RevenueCat for purchase and entitlement management, RevenueCat may receive end-user technical information and end-user transaction information, including device details, Apple receipt files, and Google purchase tokens, and RevenueCat stores customer data on AWS in the United States.

If you use a third-party app wrapper or distribution service such as Median.co, that provider may process technical information according to its own privacy policy.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • provide, operate, and maintain the App
  • store and display your nutrition logs, goals, streaks, and preferences
  • generate summaries, charts, progress views, and other insights
  • enable Pro features if you have purchased them
  • process and verify purchases and restore entitlements
  • respond to support requests
  • improve the App's performance, reliability, and usability
  • comply with legal obligations
  • prevent fraud, abuse, or misuse

3. Sensitive Personal Information

Some information you may enter into MeliusMe may be considered sensitive personal information under applicable privacy laws, including health-related information, body measurements, and nutrition-related data.

We use sensitive personal information only for App functionality, security, purchase handling, and legal compliance. We do not use it for advertising, cross-context behavioral advertising, or unrelated profiling.

For users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we ask for your explicit consent before processing health-related or biometric data in the App. By entering this data and confirming the consent prompt, you explicitly consent to the local processing and storage of that data for App functionality. You may withdraw that consent at any time by deleting the data in the App or uninstalling the App.

4. California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you may have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the CPRA, including the right to know, delete, correct, opt out of sale or sharing, and limit use of sensitive personal information. You also have the right to non-discrimination for exercising those rights.

We do not currently sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. If that changes, we will update this Privacy Policy and provide the required opt-out disclosures.

You may submit California privacy requests by contacting us at the email listed below. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

5. Other U.S. State Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have privacy rights under other U.S. state privacy laws. If your state law gives you rights to access, delete, correct, or opt out of certain processing, you may contact us to exercise those rights, and we will honor them as required by applicable law.

6. GDPR / UK GDPR Information

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process personal data only where we have a valid legal basis, which may include explicit consent, performance of a contract, legitimate interests, and legal obligation.

We use your personal data to provide the App, manage your device-level settings, process purchases, restore entitlements, provide support, maintain security, and comply with law.

If you are in the EEA or UK, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing, and request portability of your personal data, subject to applicable law. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

7. International Data Transfers

If personal data is transferred from the EEA or UK to a processor or service provider in the United States, we rely on appropriate transfer safeguards, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses where required. Where applicable, we may also use other lawful transfer mechanisms permitted under applicable law.

8. Data Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this Policy or as required by law. Our general retention periods are:

  • local app data on your device: until you delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall the App
  • support emails and support records: 24 months after your last contact
  • purchase and entitlement records: as long as needed to provide access and resolve disputes, and for up to 7 years where needed for accounting or legal compliance
  • diagnostic or error logs, if enabled: 90 days
  • exported files you create: until you delete them from your device or storage location

9. Local-First Data Storage

MeliusMe is designed so that your core nutrition and profile data remain on your device. We do not maintain a central cloud database for your personal meal logs or profile data unless a future feature explicitly states otherwise.

This means:

  • your meal history stays on your device
  • your profile and body metrics stay on your device
  • your settings stay on your device
  • your local data is controlled by you

If you delete the App or clear your device storage, local data may be permanently lost unless you exported a backup first.

10. Children's Privacy

MeliusMe is not intended for children under 13.

For users in the EEA and the United Kingdom, MeliusMe is not intended for users under 16.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the applicable age threshold. If you believe a child has provided us information in violation of this section, contact us and we will address it.

Security measures may include:

You are responsible for protecting access to your device. If someone has access to your unlocked device, they may be able to view data stored locally on that device.
